Questions? We've Got Answers.

Clear answers about creating an account, posting projects, sending proposals, communicating, and how Verilancer currently works.

Verilancer is a platform that brings businesses and people with professional skills together around project-based work. A business or client can create an account, publish a project with its description, budget, required skills and deadline, and review the proposals it receives. A professional can create a profile, add skills and profile details, browse published projects and submit a proposal for work that matches their experience. It is designed for businesses, startups, students, freelancers and independent professionals who want to find collaborators and explore practical opportunities.

Sign up using the business account type and open the project publishing area from your dashboard. Add a clear project title, explain the work and expected outcome, choose a category, enter your budget, list the skills you need and provide a deadline. Once published, the project can be discovered by professionals. You can review incoming proposals, compare each applicant's proposed amount and message, and decide whether to accept or reject an application. Take time to discuss scope, deliverables, deadlines and payment terms before beginning the work.

Create a professional account and complete your profile with an accurate description of your skills and experience. Open Find Projects to browse opportunities posted by businesses. Read the project details carefully before applying so you understand the expected work, budget and deadline. Submit a proposal with your offer and a relevant message explaining how you would approach the project. You can then track your submitted applications from My Applications and check whether each one is pending, accepted or rejected.

When a business accepts a professional's proposal, a conversation becomes available to both participants through the messaging area. They can use that conversation to clarify requirements, discuss timelines, share project-related information and coordinate the next steps. Keep important decisions in writing and make sure both sides agree on the scope, milestones, delivery expectations and payment arrangement before work starts. Messaging is currently tied to accepted proposals.

Not at this stage. Verilancer currently helps businesses and professionals discover projects, exchange proposals and communicate after a proposal is accepted; it does not currently collect, hold, release or transfer project payments. Any payment method, schedule, milestones, refunds or other financial terms must therefore be discussed and agreed directly between the two parties before work begins. Do not assume that a payment is protected by Verilancer or held in escrow. We will update this answer if in-platform payment functionality becomes available.

Users create and maintain their own profiles, so profile information should not be treated as independent identity, qualification or background verification. Before agreeing to work together, review the person's profile and relevant work samples, ask questions about their experience, confirm that the project requirements are understood, and agree on deliverables, deadlines and payment terms in writing. Share only information that is necessary for the project and use good judgement when communicating or making payment arrangements.

Start by discussing the issue clearly with the other participant through your project conversation. Refer to the scope, deadline and deliverables you both agreed on, explain what has changed or remains incomplete, and try to document a practical resolution such as a revised timeline or clarified deliverable. Verilancer does not currently provide an in-platform dispute-resolution or payment-refund process, so both parties should agree on a resolution directly. For platform-related account or technical issues, contact the Verilancer team at verilancerplatform@gmail.com.

You can create an account and set up a basic profile to start exploring Verilancer. Check the Pricing page for the current plan details before making a commitment. Premium features marked as coming soon are planned rather than currently available. Project payments are arranged directly between the client and professional at this stage, not processed by Verilancer.

We welcome questions, suggestions and honest feedback from both businesses and professionals. Email verilancerplatform@gmail.com and include enough detail for our team to understand your question. If you are reporting an issue, mention the page or feature involved and describe what happened. If you are sharing a review, tell us which part of your Verilancer experience you are describing and whether you are happy for us to publish your words and first name or preferred attribution.
